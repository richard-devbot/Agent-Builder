# Custom Web App Playbook

A guide for building a Next.js + Postgres web application — from a one-line idea to a deployed app — with the right prompts, skills, and MCP configs surfaced at each phase. **The site walks you through the sequence; you do the actual building in your IDE.**

## How it works

1. **Tell the playbook what you're building** on the intake page (a one-liner + your stack).
2. **Walk through 9 steps**, each pointing you at the prompts, skills, and MCP configs that fit that phase.
3. **Copy the prompts** into your IDE (Claude Code, Cursor, etc.) and do the work there.
4. **Mark each step complete** when you've satisfied its definition-of-done — your progress is saved locally so you can pick up where you left off.

## Who this is for

- A solo developer or small team building a new web app
- Anyone wanting a consistent, spec-driven flow across the organisation
- Engineers who want to move fast without skipping the discovery and design phases

## What this playbook does NOT do

- It does not run prompts or generate code — that happens in your IDE.
- It does not store your PRDs, stories, code, or any other content you produce. Those live in your repo, where they belong.
- It does not replace judgment — every step's "definition of done" is for you to verify in your own work.

## When NOT to use this playbook

- You're modifying an existing app — this is for greenfield.
- You're building an ETL pipeline (use the ETL playbook — coming soon).
- You're prototyping for under a day — skip steps 3, 4, and 8.
