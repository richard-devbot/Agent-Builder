# Database Migration Playbook (Cross-Engine)

An 8-phase playbook for moving a database between engines (SQL Server → Azure SQL or SQL Server → Postgres are the two we cover most) with zero or minimal downtime, comprehensive parity validation, and a clear rollback path. Click through the phases — each one points you at the right templates, skills, and MCP configs to use in your IDE.

## When to use this playbook

- You're moving from one DB engine to another (cross-engine, not just upgrading a version)
- The DB is large enough that a "stop the world, dump, restore, point" approach won't work
- You want zero or minimal downtime
- You need formal parity validation (regulatory, audit, or just "we don't trust it until we've validated")

## Critical success factors

1. **Audit before committing.** The audit reveals stored-procedure complexity, which drives most of the timeline.
2. **Validate continuously.** Don't wait until cutover to discover drift.
3. **Don't drop source until you're sure.** 30 days minimum, 90 days for critical systems.
4. **Move business logic out of DB during migration.** It's easier to migrate dumb data than smart data.
5. **Test queries on target with realistic data volume.** Performance characteristics differ between engines.

## Independent of the other playbooks

This playbook focuses on the **database layer only**. The application that talks to it is your business — you may be doing this DB migration as part of a larger app modernization (see [ASP.NET Modernization](/playbook/aspnet-to-angular-dotnet-modernization-playbook) or [WinForms Migration](/playbook/vbnet-winforms-to-spa-migration-playbook)) or as a standalone effort.
